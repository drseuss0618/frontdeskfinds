About Front Desk Finds

Front Desk Finds compares answering services, virtual receptionists and AI phone tools for owners of small businesses.

Choosing a way to answer your business calls usually means decoding pricing that is quoted by the minute, by the call or by the seat. We put those prices side by side, work out what a realistic month would cost, and point out what each vendor's page leaves unsaid.

We are an independent site run by a small business owner. We do not take payment to list or rank a product. If we later earn money from affiliate links, we say so on every page that has them. Read how we review and our affiliate disclosure.
