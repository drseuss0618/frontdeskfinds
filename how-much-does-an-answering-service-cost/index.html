How much does an answering service cost?

For a small business, a live answering service typically costs a few hundred dollars a month, an AI receptionist often costs under a hundred, and the gap comes down to who picks up the phone.

Read this first. We have not used these products hands-on. Figures below come from each vendor's own public pricing page on October 15, 2026 and may change. Vendors price by minutes, calls or seats, so the plans are not directly comparable; the worked example near the end puts them on one footing. Always confirm current prices with the vendor before you sign up.

Quick summary

  • Live human answering (Ruby, Smith.ai): roughly $250 to $2,100 a month depending on how many minutes or calls you need.
  • AI receptionist and phone systems (Quo, Allo): roughly $19 to $48 per user per month for Quo's plans and $32 to $45 for Allo's Business plan, plus separate charges for AI call handling.
  • For 40 calls of about 3 minutes each, our example works out to about $415 on Smith.ai, $720 on Ruby, and about $44 to $77 on the AI tools.

These services route your calls to trained receptionists who answer in your business name, take messages, book appointments and transfer urgent calls.

AI receptionists and phone systems

These tools are business phone systems with an AI agent that can answer calls. The AI is usually billed separately from the phone line, and an AI agent is not the same as a trained human receptionist.

What drives the bill

  • Call volume and call length. Ruby bills by minutes, Smith.ai by calls, and Quo's AI by credits per call, so a few long calls and many short calls cost differently on each.
  • Add-ons. Appointment booking, payments, recording, Spanish lines and extra transfer destinations can add noticeably to a per-call plan.
  • Human or AI. A person can handle complicated or sensitive calls. An AI agent costs a fraction of that but can miss nuance, so check how it handles calls that matter most to you.
  • Seats and numbers. Phone systems charge per user, and extra phone numbers and texting registration add small fees.
  • Billing term. Annual billing lowers the monthly rate for Quo and Allo. Month-to-month plans cost more but are easier to leave.

If your calls are high value, such as new-client inquiries, test any AI option yourself before relying on it. Rules about recording phone calls vary by state and country, so get advice before you record calls.

How to choose

  • If most calls are new leads worth hundreds or thousands of dollars each, and a missed or mishandled call is costly, a live service may pay for itself.
  • If you have few calls, mostly routine questions or after-hours messages, start with a phone system's AI and look at the transcripts for a few weeks.
  • Use free trials where offered (Quo and Allo list 7 days) and ask live services about overage and cancellation before you commit.
