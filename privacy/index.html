Privacy

Front Desk Finds does not ask you to create an account. If you fill in our start form, we collect your business name, your name, your email and the business details you type, and use them only to set up your front desk and reply to you. We do not sell them. The form is delivered to us by a form service, Web3Forms. We never ask for card numbers, passwords or Social Security numbers. To have your details deleted, email support@frontdeskfinds.com or reply "remove" to any of our emails. We do not run advertising or analytics scripts at this time.

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical information such as IP addresses and browser type when you visit.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep your message and address only to respond to you.
  • Changes. If we add analytics or other tools, we will update this page first.

Questions: support@frontdeskfinds.com.

Front Desk Finds compares answering services and AI phone tools. We also run our own AI front desk service, so we are not a neutral party, and we may earn a commission from some links, which we mark as affiliate links. See our affiliate disclosure. No vendor pays to be listed. Prices are taken from vendors' public pages and can change.
