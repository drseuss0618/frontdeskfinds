The AI front desk

We run an AI front desk for small businesses. It answers calls when no one can pick up, takes the caller's name, number and the problem, and emails the details to the owner.

What it does

  • Tells every caller it is an automated assistant.
  • Collects the caller's name, callback number (read back and confirmed) and what they need.
  • Emails you a summary after every call, including calls where the caller hangs up early.
  • Puts urgent callers through to your phone, and tells callers to hang up and call 911 for gas or fire.
  • Works in English and Spanish. Spanish is still being tested.

What it does not do

  • It does not quote prices, promise arrival times, or give repair, legal or medical advice.
  • It does not book appointments yet. Appointment requests are being tested and will be an add-on on larger plans.
  • It does not send text alerts yet; alerts are by email.

Cost

Free for 30 days, then month to month. Plans start at $99 a month, with larger plans at $199 and $349 for more calls. Setup and upkeep are included. You can cancel any time.

How we keep it separate from our reviews

  • It is not in our price comparisons, and we do not rank it against the vendors we review.
  • We tell you on every page footer that we run it, so you can weigh what we write.
  • Affiliate links, if any, are marked as such. See our affiliate disclosure.
