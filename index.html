Questions owners ask

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You forward your calls to the front desk, and you can turn the forwarding off whenever you like.

Does it replace me or my staff?

No. It picks up calls you miss, takes the details, and you call back. If a caller describes an emergency, the call is put through to your phone.

Will it quote prices or promise a time?

No. It never quotes prices, promises arrival times, or gives advice. It tells the caller you will call back.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes, it answers in the language the caller speaks. English is tested. Spanish is still being tested.

Can it book appointments?

Not yet. Taking appointment requests is being tested and will be an add-on on larger plans.

What happens to my callers' information?

It is the caller's name, number and what they need, sent to you by email. We do not sell it. Before you start, we put in writing exactly what is kept.

What if it is not for me?

The first 30 days are free. After that it is month to month and you can cancel any time.
