Answers every call

It tells callers it is an automated assistant, then collects their name, callback number and the problem.

You get the details fast

A summary lands in your email after each call, with the caller's number read back and confirmed.

Urgent calls reach you

If a caller describes an emergency, such as flooding or no water, the call is put through to your phone. For gas or fire it tells them to call 911.

English and Spanish

It answers in the language the caller speaks. English is tested; Spanish is still being tested.

How it starts

  1. You email us the basics: business name, hours, the phone number to ring for urgent calls, and where to send summaries.
  2. We set it up and look after it. There is nothing for you to install or manage.
  3. You forward your calls to it, or turn the forwarding off whenever you like.
  4. After 30 days you decide. If it is not worth it, you owe nothing.

What it costs

Free for 30 days. After that, plans start at $99 a month, month to month, and you can cancel any time. Setup and upkeep are included. Larger plans for more calls are $199 and $349 a month; ask us which fits.

Plain limits

  • It does not quote prices, promise arrival times, or give advice. It tells the caller you will call back.
  • It is early. We are starting with a small number of shops, and we will tell you what is not built yet.
  • Alerts come by email. Text alerts are not available yet.
  • Check your state's rules about recorded calls and automated assistants before you go live; we will go over this with you. This is not legal advice.
