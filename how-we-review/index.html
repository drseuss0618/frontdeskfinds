How we review

What we do, what we don't, and how we keep money from shaping what we say.

What we do

  • We read each vendor's public pricing, plan and terms pages and record the figures with the date we checked.
  • We work out example costs from those figures and show our arithmetic so you can check it.
  • When a vendor's page is unclear or leaves something out, such as overage charges, we say so rather than guessing.

What we do not do (yet)

  • We have not tested these products hands-on unless a page says we have. Until a page says otherwise, treat it as a price and feature comparison, not a performance review.
  • We do not claim that any vendor is the best. Which service fits depends on your call volume, budget and how much each call is worth.

Independence

  • No vendor pays to be listed, ranked or described. We choose vendors by relevance to small businesses.
  • We may earn a commission when you click an affiliate link and buy. Each such link is marked, and earning a commission will not change our figures or conclusions. Details are in our affiliate disclosure.
  • We also run our own AI front desk service. We do not rank it against the vendors we review, and it does not appear in our price comparisons. See our service.

Corrections

Prices change. If something is wrong or out of date, email support.daybreakapp@gmail.com and we will check it and update the page, noting the date.
